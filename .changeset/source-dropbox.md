---
"@ifc-lite/source-dropbox": minor
---

Adds a Dropbox file-source provider, authenticating via the new OAuth support in `@ifc-lite/plugin-api` (`manifest.auth: 'oauth'` + `PluginContext.getAccessToken`). Browse and load IFC files directly from a connected Dropbox account through the same Sources panel as `@ifc-lite/source-dalux`.
